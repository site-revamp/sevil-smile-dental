Sevil Smile Studio — Homepage Redesign Documentation

1. Website Overview

Sevil Smile Studio is a multi-location dental clinic brand operating in Türkiye (Istanbul, Antalya, and Didim). Operating within the dental healthcare, cosmetic dentistry, and dental tourism sectors, the business serves both domestic and international patients seeking procedures ranging from Digital Smile Design and cosmetic veneers to full mouth rehabilitations and dental implants.

Business Purpose & Target Audience

The primary goal of the website is to attract, inform, and convert patients interested in cosmetic and restorative dental treatments, particularly international medical tourists seeking comprehensive treatment travel packages, airport transfers, and English-speaking patient support.

Homepage Redesign Direction

The new homepage design provides an authentic, high-converting digital storefront. It modernizes the visual presentation, streamlines clinical and logistical information, and establishes a seamless conversion pathway for domestic and international patients.

2. Main Improvements

Fix 1: Unified International Patient Onboarding & Booking Pathways

Previous Issue: International patients faced unclear navigation steps when attempting to coordinate travel, consultations, and booking across multiple clinic locations.

What Was Changed: Integrated a streamlined conversion flow highlighting direct international contact channels (UK WhatsApp: +44 745 819 7011, direct treatment form links), alongside explicitly detailed international patient services (treatment packages, accommodation coordination, and airport transfers).

Impact: Provides immediate clarity and low-friction booking channels tailored specifically to medical tourists.

Fix 2: Standardized Multi-Location Clinic & Leadership Transparency

Previous Issue: Multi-clinic contact data (Istanbul, Antalya, Didim) and company background details were fragmented, leading to ambiguity regarding founding history (2008 vs. 20+ years experience claims).

What Was Changed: Structured clear, location-specific contact cards (including exact addresses and phone lines for Istanbul, Antalya, and Didim) and explicitly highlighted verified leadership (Founder Sevil Pekmezci, CEO Berfin Gonca Yetim, and Head Dentist Aybüke Demir).

Impact: Establishes organizational transparency, reinforces clinical credibility, and allows patients to easily contact their location of choice.

Fix 3: Categorized Clinical Services & Technology Showcase

Previous Issue: Advanced restorative options, CAD/CAM tech, and premium implant offerings were not clearly structured, obscuring the studio's technical capabilities.

What Was Changed: Restructured the treatments section to explicitly feature specific procedures (All-on-4/6, E-max Veneers, Zirconia Crowns, Composite Bonding) alongside partner implant brands (Straumann, MIS, Neodent, Astra Tech) and digital workflow capabilities (CAD/CAM, Intraoral Scanning, Digital Smile Design).

Impact: Builds patient trust by showcasing high-tier technology and material standards upfront.

3. Design Improvements

Visual Presentation & Layout: Modernized the interface with clean, high-contrast visual hierarchy, prioritizing clinical results and service offerings.

User Experience (UX): Improved navigation paths with prominent Call-To-Action (CTA) buttons linking directly to the online consultation form (/treatmentform) and multi-channel support.

Consistency & Appearance: Standardized visual branding across location details, leadership profiles, social media links, and service modules for a cohesive brand identity.
